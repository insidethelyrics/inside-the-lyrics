A few defining eras

1950s–1960s

Modern rumba emerges

Early artists and orchestras develop a modern popular sound that becomes a defining cultural force.

1970s

Orchestras flourish

Band culture and virtuoso musicianship help produce an exceptionally creative period in Congolese music.

1980s onward

Soukous travels

Dance-driven guitar music reaches audiences across Africa and around the world, continuing to evolve.
