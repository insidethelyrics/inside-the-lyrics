Our mission

Listen beyond the melody

Inside the Lyrics with Sir Wakho explores the stories behind African music. We celebrate influential artists, unpack the meaning and context of songs, and help preserve musical heritage for curious listeners and future generations.

Our vision

A living music archive

We want to make African music history easier to discover and enjoy—a welcoming digital space connecting artists, bands, songs, places, and the generations who keep the music alive.
