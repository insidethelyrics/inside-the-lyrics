🎸 The OK Jazz Musicians Directory

OK Jazz was shaped by generations of singers, guitarists, percussionists and instrumentalists. This directory brings together musicians named in the Inside the Lyrics OK Jazz archive.

About this directory

This is a historical reference based on our OK Jazz research notes. It is not a claim to include every musician who ever performed with the orchestra.

Where the archive does not specify an instrument or role, this directory says so rather than guessing.

📝 Historical notes requiring verification

The research notes contain spelling variations such as Liberlin/Libertin, Roitelet/Roitalet, Lola Checain/Chacain, and different spellings of Dialtho.

Roitelet is recorded as leaving in 1960 but also appears in later line-up lists. Michael Boyibanda's arrival is dated 1963 in one place and 1964 in another.

These details are flagged rather than silently reconciled. Further documentary evidence can be used to update the directory.
