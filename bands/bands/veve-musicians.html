Orchestra Vévé: Musicians and Personnel

The voices, instruments and personalities of the Vévé era

Orchestra Vévé's history was shaped by Verckys Kiamuangana Mateta and a wider community of singers and instrumentalists. This archive documents the musicians associated with the orchestra and the musical paths they followed.

The founder

1. Verckys Kiamuangana Mateta

Georges Kiamuangana Mateta

Known as: Verckys

Roles: Saxophonist, composer, bandleader, producer and music entrepreneur.

Verckys was the central figure behind Orchestra Vévé. His saxophone playing and musical leadership gave the orchestra its identity.

Before establishing the group in 1969, he had gained experience in Kinshasa's music scene and performed with OK Jazz under Franco Luambo.

His influence extended beyond performance into recording, production and artist development through the wider Éditions Vévé enterprise.

Verckys died on 13 October 2022 in Kinshasa.

The voices of the orchestra

2. Vocalists Associated with Orchestra Vévé

The orchestra's early vocal identity was shaped by a number of singers whose individual voices helped distinguish its recordings and performances.

Matadidi Mario

Matadidi Mario was one of the prominent vocalists associated with the early Orchestra Vévé formation.

He became particularly well known through his association with Djeskain and Sinatra in the vocal combination remembered as Trio Madjesi.

Marcel Loko Massengo – Djeskain

Marcel Loko Massengo, known as Djeskain, was another important vocalist associated with the early orchestra.

His stage identity contributed to the name Trio Madjesi, alongside Matadidi Mario and Sinatra.

Bonghat Tsekabu – Saak-Sakul / Sinatra

Bonghat Tsekabu is associated with the stage names Saak-Sakul and Sinatra in historical accounts of the orchestra.

He was one of the three singers associated with Trio Madjesi. His contribution forms part of the group's early vocal history.

Bovic Bondo

Bovic Bondo is also reported among the vocalists associated with the early Orchestra Vévé.

More detailed recording credits and the precise period of his participation require further confirmation.

A memorable vocal combination

3. Trio Madjesi

Trio Madjesi became one of the most recognisable vocal combinations associated with Orchestra Vévé. Its name is linked to three singers:

Ma Matadidi Mario
Dje Djeskain
Si Sinatra / Saak-Sakul

Why Trio Madjesi mattered

The three singers brought together different vocal personalities. Their combination became an important part of the orchestra's public identity.

Their later independent direction and association with Orchestra Sosoliso demonstrate how singers could develop beyond the group in which they first gained recognition.

Trio Madjesi should be understood as a distinct vocal identity, not as the complete name of Orchestra Vévé.

The instrumental section

4. Instrumentalists Associated with the Early Orchestra

Historical personnel accounts identify several instrumentalists associated with the early Orchestra Vévé formation. The surviving information is incomplete, and some names appear primarily as stage names.

Danila

Reported role: Lead guitarist.

Danila is identified in some personnel accounts as a lead guitarist associated with the orchestra.

Further details about his biography and individual recordings remain to be established.

Jim

Reported role: Bassist.

Jim is named among the early instrumentalists associated with Orchestra Vévé. His reported role was bass guitar.

Maproco

Reported role: Saxophonist.

Maproco is identified in some historical personnel accounts as a saxophonist.

The exact period of his participation and his recording credits require additional verification.

Other instrumental contributors

Like many Congolese orchestras of the period, Orchestra Vévé's instrumental personnel changed over time. Additional guitarists, bassists, percussionists and horn players may be identified through original record labels, photographs and recording documentation.

We will add names to this section only when there is sufficient evidence connecting them to the orchestra.

Understanding the sound

5. The Musicians and Their Musical Roles

Orchestra Vévé's musical identity depended on the interaction between its vocalists and instrumentalists.

Saxophone

Verckys's saxophone playing was one of the most distinctive elements of the orchestra's musical identity.

Lead guitar

The lead guitar provided melodic phrases and instrumental responses around the vocal sections.

Bass guitar

The bass supported the rhythmic foundation and helped maintain the movement of the dance arrangements.

Vocals

The singers carried the lyrical and melodic identity of the orchestra. Their individual styles and vocal combinations helped make its recordings memorable.

Beyond Orchestra Vévé

6. Musicians and Their Later Careers

Trio Madjesi and Orchestra Sosoliso

The three singers associated with Trio Madjesi later became connected with Orchestra Sosoliso.

This movement illustrates the changing relationships between Congolese musicians, who frequently formed new groups after leaving established orchestras.

Verckys and Éditions Vévé

Verckys increasingly devoted attention to recording, production and the development of other artists.

His work through Éditions Vévé connected him with a wider musical network, but artists recorded under the label should not automatically be listed as Orchestra Vévé members.

Research standards

7. Personnel and Attribution Notes

  • Verckys is identified as the founder and central bandleader of Orchestra Vévé.
  • Matadidi Mario, Djeskain and Sinatra are associated with the early vocal identity and Trio Madjesi.
  • Bovic Bondo is reported among the early vocalists, but detailed recording credits require confirmation.
  • Danila, Jim and Maproco are included with their reported instrumental roles, not as a fully verified permanent lineup.
  • Trio Madjesi is distinguished from Orchestra Vévé and its later association with Orchestra Sosoliso.
  • Artists connected with Éditions Vévé are not automatically treated as Orchestra Vévé members.
  • More personnel will be added as reliable recording credits and historical photographs are examined.
