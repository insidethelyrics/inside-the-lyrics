4. Sources on Recordings and Discography

Analog Africa – Verckys et l'Orchestre Vévé

The 2014 retrospective collection titled Verckys et l'Orchestre Vévé: Congolese Funk, Afrobeat and Psychedelic Rumba 1969–1978 is an important reference for selected recordings associated with Verckys and the orchestra.

Its track selection includes Bassala Hot, Ya Nini, Cheka Sana, Oui Verckys, Nakobala Yo Denise, Sex Vévé, Sisa Motema, Talali Talala, Zonga Vonvon, Nakomi Paralise and Matinda Comono.

How we document history

6. Editorial Standards and Historical Cautions

The Orchestra Vévé archive is a developing historical resource. We recognise that older Congolese recordings and band histories can be difficult to reconstruct because of incomplete documentation, alternate spellings, regional releases and conflicting recollections.

Formation date

The archive uses 1969 as the general formation year of Orchestra Vévé. More precise dates found in individual accounts are not treated as universally established.

Éditions Vévé foundation date

Published accounts differ on whether the label was established around 1970 or in 1974. We therefore describe its development as taking place during the early 1970s.

Trio Madjesi chronology

The trio is associated with singers who emerged from Orchestra Vévé. Accounts place the creation and public emergence of Orchestra Sosoliso in 1972. The archive avoids assigning unsupported exact dates to every stage of the trio's development.

Musician membership

Personnel lists may combine musicians from different periods. We distinguish confirmed roles from reported associations and avoid presenting every name as part of one permanent lineup.

Recordings and labels

A later compilation is not the same thing as an original album. Song titles, original release dates, label names and catalogue numbers require individual verification.

Verckys and Orchestra Vévé

Verckys's solo recordings are distinguished from recordings credited to Orchestra Vévé. Likewise, artists associated with Éditions Vévé are not automatically classified as members of the orchestra.

An evolving archive

7. Help Preserve the History

We welcome additional historical information, original record labels, photographs, concert programmes and credible first-hand recollections that can help improve this archive.

Our aim is to preserve the history of Congolese music while giving proper recognition to the artists, instrumentalists, producers and cultural figures who shaped it.

All substantial additions should include their source or supporting evidence so that the archive remains transparent and historically responsible.
