About Our Research

This archive brings together historical writing, music research, recording catalogues, discographical information and published accounts of African Jazz and its musicians.

Our main subject is the orchestra led by Joseph Athanase Tshamala Kabasele, popularly known as Grand Kallé, and the musical world that developed around it.

Historical accounts do not always agree on dates, membership, recording credits and the final years of the orchestra. Where possible, this archive preserves those differences rather than presenting uncertain details as established facts.

6. Research Limitations and Editorial Standards

Historical music research often involves incomplete recording labels, inconsistent spellings, missing session documentation and conflicting oral accounts. The African Jazz archive therefore follows these editorial principles:

  • Dates are presented as approximate when sources disagree.
  • Early studio musicians are not automatically described as permanent orchestra members.
  • Recording appearances are distinguished from confirmed band membership.
  • Song titles are preserved with known spelling variations where appropriate.
  • Original releases are distinguished from later compilations and reissues.
  • Claims about disputed events are identified as disputed rather than presented as settled history.
  • Related orchestras are treated as separate musical organisations even when they share musicians or origins.

Our Commitment to Musical Heritage

Inside the Lyrics with Sir Wakho is dedicated to preserving African musical history through research, storytelling and accessible documentation.

We recognise that the history of African music is larger than the surviving records. This archive remains open to corrections, additional historical evidence, family collections, original record sleeves and testimonies from people who witnessed these musical developments.
