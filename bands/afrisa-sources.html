OUR RESEARCH APPROACH

Documenting Afrisa International

This archive documents the history of Afrisa International, the orchestra led by Tabu Ley Rochereau. It draws on music scholarship, historical reporting, discographical references and retrospective accounts.

The purpose of this page is to make the research process transparent. Historical dates, personnel credits and recording details are not always consistent across published accounts.

Where sources differ, the archive uses cautious wording rather than presenting uncertain information as established fact.

EDITORIAL NOTES

Dates and Historical Distinctions

Formation and Naming

African Fiesta National was the direct predecessor of Afrisa International. ISA was established in 1969, while the Afrisa identity is generally associated with the period around 1970.

Some accounts give different dates for the transition. The archive therefore distinguishes the establishment of ISA from the emergence of the Afrisa name.

African Fiesta Sukisa

African Fiesta Sukisa was led by Dr. Nico after the separation of African Fiesta. It was a separate successor branch and should not be confused with Afrisa.

Musician Membership

Afrisa's personnel changed over several decades. A musician's association with the orchestra does not automatically establish that the musician belonged to every lineup or recorded every song.

Recording Dates and Reissues

Overseas compilations and retrospective releases sometimes combine songs that originally appeared as separate singles. Original recording dates and reissue dates must therefore be distinguished.

Later Afrisa Formation

The end of the classic Kinshasa orchestra period should not be confused with the end of all Afrisa activity. A smaller formation continued internationally and recorded for Rounder Records.

OUR COMMITMENT

Preserving the Musical Record

Inside the Lyrics with Sir Wakho aims to preserve African musical heritage through careful historical documentation.

This archive remains open to correction, additional evidence and contributions from researchers, musicians, collectors and listeners with reliable historical material.

Record sleeves, original releases, interviews and archival photographs are especially valuable for improving the accuracy of this history.
