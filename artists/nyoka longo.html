🎸 Nyoka Longo

Early Zaïko Langa Langa vocalist and co-founder

Josart Nyoka Longo was one of the early figures associated with the formation of Zaïko Langa Langa and became an important voice in the band’s earliest phase.

👤 Artist Profile

Full Name: Josart Nyoka Longo

Known As: Nyoka Longo

Born: 7 September 1953

Country: Democratic Republic of Congo 🇨🇩

Genres: Congolese Rumba, Soukous

Primary Affiliation: Zaïko Langa Langa

Known For: Early vocalist, co-founder, and a central figure in the emergence of Zaïko Langa Langa.

🌱 Early Career

Nyoka Longo appears in the source notes as an early vocalist and co-founder of Zaïko Langa Langa. The notes place him among the musicians identified as part of the group’s founding generation.

He was associated with the early performance and vocal identity of Zaïko during the period when the band was emerging from the broader Congolese popular music scene.

🎶 Relationship to Zaïko Langa Langa

The research notes describe Nyoka Longo as an early vocalist and co-founder of Zaïko Langa Langa. He is also noted as a musician who later released La Tout Neige.

In the archive, he is retained as a founding-era figure whose role is documented in the early notes, even where later details still need verification.

📌 Key Archive Note

The note in the Zaïko archive records: Josart Nyoka Longo — Early vocalist and co-founder; later released La Tout Neige.
