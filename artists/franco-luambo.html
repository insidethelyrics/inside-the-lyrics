🎸 Franco Luambo

The Wizard of the Guitar

The legendary musician who defined the sound of Congolese Rumba and changed African music forever.

👤 Artist Profile

Full Name: François Luambo Makiadi

Stage Name: Franco Luambo

Born: 6 December 1938

Died: 12 October 1989

Country: Democratic Republic of Congo 🇨🇩

Genres: Congolese Rumba, Soukous

Main Band: OK Jazz

Nickname: Le Sorcier de la Guitare (The Wizard of the Guitar)

🎵 Introduction

Franco Luambo Makiadi was one of Africa's greatest musicians and one of the most influential figures in the history of Congolese music.

Through his powerful guitar style, songwriting and leadership of OK Jazz, Franco helped transform Congolese Rumba into one of Africa's most respected musical movements.

His songs were more than entertainment. They told stories about love, society, relationships, culture and everyday African life.
