🎷 Verckys Kiamuangana

The Saxophone Giant of Congolese Music

A legendary Congolese saxophonist, composer, bandleader and music entrepreneur who became one of the defining figures of the golden era of Congolese popular music.

👤 Artist Profile

Full Name: Georges Kiamuangana Mateta

Known As: Verckys Kiamuangana

Born: 19 May 1944

Died: 13 October 2022

Country: Democratic Republic of Congo 🇨🇩

Instrument: Saxophone

Genres: Congolese Rumba, Soukous

Major Band: Orchestre Vévé

Known For: Saxophone playing, composition, band leadership and developing the careers of younger Congolese musicians.

🎷 The Saxophone

Verckys became one of the most recognizable saxophonists in Congolese popular music.

His powerful saxophone sound became an important part of the orchestral character of Congolese Rumba and helped give his recordings a distinctive identity.

At a time when orchestras were the centre of African popular music, the saxophone was one of the instruments capable of adding dramatic melodies and powerful instrumental responses to the guitar-driven rhythm.

🎺 Orchestre Vévé

Verckys founded Orchestre Vévé, which became an important institution in the history of Congolese popular music.

The orchestra became known for its energetic arrangements and helped introduce and develop a new generation of musicians.
